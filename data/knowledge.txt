Project DARWIN is a local-first agentic voice assistant created by Ashish.

DARWIN stands for Distributed Agentic Reasoning and Workflow Intelligence Network.

DARWIN uses OpenAI GPT-OSS-120B through Groq as its primary reasoning model.
It uses Qwen3 14B through Ollama as its offline fallback model.

LangGraph manages conversation flow and agent orchestration.
SQLite provides persistent conversation memory.
ChromaDB stores and retrieves unstructured knowledge.
Faster Whisper provides local speech recognition.
Fish Audio S2.1 provides streamed text-to-speech, with pyttsx3 as a local fallback.
